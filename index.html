How it works

Camera, confirm, done

The whole thing takes about as long as finding the card in your wallet would have.

What makes it work

The details that decide whether it scans

👁️

Reads the card, not just the barcode

Name, number and expiry recognised on device, then shown to you to correct before anything is created.

📶

Barcodes that really scan

Wallet can draw four formats. The eight it can't — EAN-13, Code 39 and the rest — are drawn faithfully onto the pass instead.

🎨

Looks like your card

The pass takes your card's own colours from the photo. Templates recognise the kind of card, 18 brands get their own palette, and you can add a logo.

⏰

Reminded before it lapses

A month before, a week before, and on the day a card expires — set up by reading your card, not by you.

👨‍👩‍👧

Share a household card

One library or leisure card, both of you carrying it. Off by default, because a pass that forwards from the lock screen is a surprise.

☁️

Backed up, if you want

Optional iCloud backup through your own account, so a new phone doesn't mean scanning everything again.

The photo never leaves your phone

Your card is read on your device and the photo is discarded — never saved, never uploaded. Only the details needed to lay out a pass are sent to be signed, and nothing is kept afterwards. No account required.

Tessera creates a personal digital copy of a card you already own. It is not an official credential and does not produce government identity documents. Some places will still ask for the physical card.
