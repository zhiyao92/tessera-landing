Tessera

Your cards, in Apple Wallet

Point your camera at a membership, loyalty or club card. Tessera reads it on your device, you confirm the details, and it becomes a real Apple Wallet pass — with a barcode that scans like the original.

Coming to the App Store

What it does

Reads the card, not just the barcode

Name, number, expiry — recognised on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework, then shown to you to correct before anything is created.

Barcodes that really scan

Wallet draws a fresh code from the number behind yours. Types Wallet can't draw — EAN-13, Code 39 — are drawn faithfully onto the pass instead.

Looks like your card

The pass takes your card's own colours from the photo. Templates recognise the kind of card, popular brands get their palette, and you can add a logo.

Reminded before it lapses

A month before, a week before, and on the day a card expires — set up by reading your card, not by you.

Kept, backed up, shared

Optional iCloud backup through your own account. Share a household card so both of you carry it.

The right kind of pass

Membership cards, coupons and season tickets each become the proper Wallet layout, not one-size-fits-all.

Built private, and honest about it

The photo of your card is read on your device and then discarded — never saved, never uploaded. Only the details needed to lay out a pass are sent to be signed, and nothing is kept afterwards. No account required.

Tessera creates a personal digital copy of a card you already own. It is not an official credential and does not produce government identity documents. Some places will still ask for the physical card.
